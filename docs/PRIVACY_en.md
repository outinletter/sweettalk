SweetTalk Privacy Policy

Effective: October 15, 2026. Contact: outintube@gmail.com

No account, ads or tracking. Characters, history, translation cache, entered area, language and reminder settings are stored on your device. The developer does not operate a conversation-history database.

With explicit consent, messages, character settings and AI replies are sent through Cloudflare to the DeepSeek API for generation, translation and input/output safety checks. Without consent, AI features are unavailable; settings and policies remain accessible. Withdraw consent in settings.

Weather is off by default. If enabled, your entered area is sent to wttr.in; the area and weather context are sent to DeepSeek. GPS permission is not requested. Providers may receive connection IP addresses. When enabled, Cloudflare rate-limit KV stores IP-based counts for approximately 24 hours.

DeepSeek’s general policy does not directly specify downstream developer-app processing rules. API retention, processing region and exclusion from training are not confirmed. We do not guarantee zero retention or exclusion from training. Do not enter sensitive data. Contact the operator for privacy or deletion requests; the operator will request provider assistance where needed.

Delete individual chats or characters, or use Delete all app data in home settings. Full deletion clears app-managed local history, cache and scheduled reminders, retaining the selected language. Previously processed provider data and exported backups are not deleted by this action. Delete backups separately. Reminders are local; report emails are sent by you through your email app.
